About AdventureWorks

Business background

Adventure Works Cycles is a fictional, large multinational bicycle manufacturer. It makes and sells metal and composite bikes to North American, European and Asian markets, along with components, clothing and accessories. Its sales come through two channels: resellers (bike shops and retail partners buying in volume) and internet (direct to consumers). AdventureWorksDW2020 is the data warehouse built for reporting on these sales. It uses a star schema, with fact tables holding the transactions and dimension tables holding the descriptive context, and the product dimension is snowflaked into subcategory and category.

Important tables pre-selected in this diagram

FactResellerSales fact
One row per reseller order line (SalesOrderNumber + SalesOrderLineNumber). Holds order quantity, unit price, total product cost and sales amount, and links to product, reseller, employee, promotion, sales territory and the order, due and ship dates.
DimProduct dimension
The product catalogue: name, model, colour, size, standard cost, list price, dealer price and product line. Each product belongs to a subcategory.
DimProductSubcategory dimension
The middle level of the product hierarchy, for example Mountain Bikes, Road Bikes, Helmets or Jerseys. It links each product to its category.
DimProductCategory dimension
The top level of the product hierarchy: Bikes, Components, Clothing and Accessories. Together with the subcategory it makes the snowflake.
DimReseller dimension
The businesses that buy for resale: business type (such as Warehouse, Value Added Reseller or Specialty Bike Shop), product line, number of employees, year opened and annual sales and revenue.
DimEmployee dimension
Staff, including the sales people who handle reseller orders: name, title, department, hire date and a sales-person flag. ParentEmployeeKey points to the employee's manager, which gives the reporting hierarchy.
DimSalesTerritory dimension
The sales regions: region, country and group (North America, Europe, Pacific), used to compare performance geographically.
DimEmployeeSalesTerritory bridge
Resolves the many-to-many link between employees and territories, since a sales person can cover several territories and a territory can have several sales people. Its key is both EmployeeKey and SalesTerritoryKey.

Other tables in the warehouse, not pre-selected, include FactInternetSales (online orders), DimCustomer, DimDate, DimPromotion, DimCurrency, DimGeography and the finance tables.

Key performance metrics

  • Sales amount Sum of SalesAmount, the total reseller revenue.
  • Total product cost Sum of TotalProductCost, what the goods sold cost to produce.
  • Gross margin Sales amount minus total product cost.
  • Gross margin % Gross margin divided by sales amount; shows how profitable each product line is.
  • Units sold Sum of OrderQuantity.
  • Average selling price Sales amount divided by units sold; compare it with ListPrice and DealerPrice to see discounting.
  • Orders and average order value Distinct count of SalesOrderNumber, and sales amount divided by that count.
  • Sales by dimension Any of the above broken down by product category, reseller business type, sales territory or sales person, and trended by order date.
